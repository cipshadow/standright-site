StandRight is a private posture habit on Android. When you unlock, one tap logs your own check-in, and the answers stay on your phone. It asks again if you stay on the phone. Answer Upright or Slouched, right in the notification. Nothing opens.

<b>Runs on your schedule.</b> Pick your days, your hours, and the gap between check-ins. Need quiet for an hour? One tap pauses everything, and it resumes on its own.

<b>Phone tilt glow.</b> Reading in bed with the phone tipped back? Past your angle, a soft glow breathes at the screen edges until you lift it and sit up straight. Never a flash, never an alarm. It runs only for a short glow window after each unlock, so it can't nag all evening. It measures the phone, not your body.

<b>See the habit build.</b> Four weeks as a calendar, each day coloured by your check-ins and how often the phone dipped. Today's check-ins on a timeline, a streak once you have one, and one quiet evening recap. Never a guilt trip. Your own check-ins become something you can see.

<b>Tuned to real life.</b> Wind-down stops the check-ins in the last hour of your day. Swiping a check-in away counts as "can't right now", not a failure.

<b>Private by design.</b> No account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission. Your data stays on your phone, apart from Android's own device backup, and you can export all of it as CSV.

StandRight is a habit tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. If you have back or neck pain, talk to a healthcare professional.
