# Posture reminder apps for Android, compared by their Play listings

{% include updated.html %}

This page is for a private habit: a check-in you answer yourself, and a listing you can read before you install. It was written by the maker of StandRight, listed on Google Play as ChipChopInc. StandRight is one row. It is held to the same rule as the others.

The rule is narrow. Each cell is as the developer declares on Google Play, from the United States listing in English, checked on 26 November 2026. The "best for" line only restates the trigger that listing describes. It is not a ranking, and it is not a statement about how well any app works. Health claims in the listings are left out.

If the listing did not state a timer, an unlock, or a tilt, the trigger cell says so. Account requirements and permissions are left out. Download figures are the ranges printed on the listing, not exact install counts.
