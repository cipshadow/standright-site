Sit taller, gently.

Gentle posture check-ins every time you unlock your Android phone. One tap to answer, a soft glow when the phone tips back, and a quiet picture of your posture over time.

Coming soon to Google Play

A check-in at the moment you slouch

Every phone unlock is a moment you're probably slouching. StandRight turns that exact moment into a habit: a silent heads-up check-in when you unlock, then again if you stay on the phone, answered with one tap, "Upright" or "Slouched." Answering happens right in the notification; nothing opens.

Runs on your schedule

Pick which days it's active, your hours, and the minimum gap between check-ins. Need quiet for an hour? One tap pauses everything and it resumes itself.

Straight-up mode

Reading in bed with the phone tipped back? Past your chosen angle, a soft glow breathes at the screen edges until you straighten up. Never a flash, never an alarm. It only runs for a few minutes after each unlock, so it cannot nag all evening.

See the habit build

Four weeks as a calendar, each day coloured by your check-ins blended with how often the phone tipped back. Today's check-ins on a timeline, a streak once you have one, and one quiet evening recap. Never a guilt trip.

Tuned to real life

Wind-down stops the asking in the last hour of your day. Swiping a check-in away counts as "can't right now," not a failure, and it is recorded as its own answer.

Private by design

No account. No ads. No analytics. No servers of ours. Your data stays on your phone, aside from Android's own device backup, and you can export all of it as CSV whenever you like. Details in the privacy policy.

StandRight is a habit-building reminder tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. If you have back or neck pain, talk to a healthcare professional.
