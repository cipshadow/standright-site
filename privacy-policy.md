# MobilePosture — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-08-26**

MobilePosture ("the app") is a posture habit reminder app for Android,
developed and published by Cip Blujdea (contact: cipblujdea95@gmail.com).
This policy describes what data the app handles and where it goes. The short
version: everything stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

The app records your posture check-in answers, timed-out and dismissed
reminders, tilt ("straight-up mode") events, and your settings. All of this is
stored only in a local database and preferences file on your device. It is
never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere by the app.

## What the app collects or shares

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no
analytics, and no third-party SDKs that transmit data. It makes no network
requests. We (the developer) never see your data.

## CSV export

The History screen lets you export your data as CSV files. Export uses the
Android share sheet: your data goes only to the app or destination you choose
in that moment. Nothing is exported without your explicit action.

## Device backups

Android may include the app's local data in your device's own cloud backup or
device-to-device transfer (a feature of your Google account and device, not of
the app). You can exclude the app from backups in Android's settings.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Notifications** — to show posture reminders and the daily recap.
- **Display over other apps** (optional) — for the brief on-screen tilt cue.
- **Gravity/accelerometer sensor** — read on-device, screen-on only, to detect
  when the phone is tilted back. Sensor readings are processed locally and only
  tilt events (time, duration, angle) are stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for users 18 and over.

## Not medical advice

MobilePosture is a habit-building reminder tool, not a medical device. It does
not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data is handled (for example,
adding crash reporting), this policy will be updated before that version ships,
with a new effective date.
